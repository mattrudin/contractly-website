Privacy

Technical information about the app and this website. Updated: 14 October 2026.

On-device storage

Contractly stores contracts, documents and settings locally. The app has no user account and no configured CloudKit sync. Device backups and storage destinations you choose may contain data outside your device.

Backups and sharing

Exported backups contain contract data, documents and settings in plain text, without their own encryption. Your chosen export or sharing destination determines who can access them. Restoring a backup replaces the current data.

Reminders and external connections

With your permission, the app creates local notifications and entries in Apple Calendar or Reminders. These entries may sync through your configured accounts. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter; the request contains no contract data. Purchases use Apple StoreKit. Opening saved links connects your browser to the relevant provider.

This website

This website uses no analytics, cookies or externally loaded fonts. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub logs IP addresses for security purposes. External pages are opened only when you follow a link.

Contact

Sending an email shares your address and message with the recipient. Include only information needed for your request. Questions or feedback? Find the contact option in the app under Settings → “Missing something?”.
