Good to know

How much does Contractly cost?

Manage up to four contracts for free. Premium removes this limit. See the App Store or purchase dialog for the current price and purchase terms.

Can I back up my data?

Yes. Use Backup & Restore to export and restore contracts, documents and settings. Exported backups are not encrypted: keep them in a safe place.
